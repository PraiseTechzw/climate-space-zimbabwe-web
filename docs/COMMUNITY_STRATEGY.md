# Community Strategy: Membership & Clubs

## 1. Philosophy: "Grassroots Growth"
Climate action cannot be top-down. It must grow from the ground up—in classrooms, villages, and campuses. Our membership model is designed to be accessible, active, and empowering.

## 2. Who Can Join?
Climate Space is open to everyone who shares our vision for a sustainable Zimbabwe.
*   **Junior Members (Under 18):** Primary and High School students (must join via a School Club).
*   **Full Members (18+):** University students, professionals, farmers, and activists.
*   **Institutional Members:** Schools, Universities, and Corporate Partners.

## 3. Climate Clubs (The Engine of the Movement)
We operate through a network of decentralized chapters called **Climate Space Clubs**.

### Types of Clubs
1.  **School Clubs:** Led by a teacher patron + student executive. Focus: Education, tree planting, recycling.
2.  **Uni Chapters:** Led by student guilds. Focus: Research, innovation, advocacy campaigns.
3.  **Community Hubs:** Led by local elders or youth leaders. Focus: Practical adaptation (gardens, water harvesting).

### How Clubs Work
*   **Structure:** Each club must have a Chairperson, Secretary, and Treasurer.
*   **Curriculum:** Climate Space HQ provides a "Club Starter Pack" with monthly activity guides (e.g., "Month 1: Waste Audit", "Month 2: Climate Art").
*   **Reporting:** Clubs submit a simple monthly impact report (photos + stats) to HQ via the App.

## 4. Benefits of Joining
*   **For Individuals:**
    *   **Access:** Use of the AI Agri-Search Engine and Digital Library.
    *   **Network:** Connect with mentors and peers across Zimbabwe.
    *   **Recognition:** Digital certificates for volunteer hours.
*   **For Clubs:**
    *   **Resources:** Free seeds, educational posters, and tree saplings (when available).
    *   **Competitions:** Entry into the annual "Green Innovation Challenge."

## 5. Member Responsibilities
Being a member is a commitment, not just a badge.
*   **Act:** Participate in at least one major climate action per term/season.
*   **Learn:** Complete one module of the Climate Literacy Course annually.
*   **Share:** Spread verified climate information (no fake news).

## 6. How to Start a Club (5 Steps)
1.  **Gather:** Find 5 interested members and 1 patron (if school-based).
2.  **Register:** Submit the "New Chapter Application" online or via WhatsApp.
3.  **Orient:** Attend a virtual or physical induction with a Climate Space coordinator.
4.  **Launch:** Host a launch event (e.g., a cleanup or planting day).
5.  **Sustain:** Hold monthly meetings and execute projects.

## 7. Tone & Culture
*   **We are Doers:** We don't just talk in meetings; we work in the soil.
*   **We are Inclusive:** No one is too young or too old to lead.
*   **We are Solution-Focused:** We acknowledge problems but obsess over solutions.
