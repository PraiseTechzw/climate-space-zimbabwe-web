# Creative Space Strategy

## 1. Purpose: "The Heart of the Data"
The Creative Space is where statistics meet soul. While our AI provides the *logic* for adaptation, the Creative Space provides the *motivation*.
**Goal:** To make climate change understandable, personal, and emotional through art. To document the Zimbabwean reality—both the struggle and the beauty—and inspire a culture of stewardship.

## 2. Allowed Content Types
We accept submissions that tell a story about the environment.
*   **Visual Art:** Digital paintings, sketches, and graphic design posters.
*   **Photography:**
    *   *The Reality:* Documentation of droughts, floods, or pollution.
    *   *The Hope:* Images of regeneration, successful harvests, or green beauty.
*   **Written Word:** Poetry, short essays, and "letters to the future."
*   **Multimedia:** Short films (under 5 mins), spoken word audio, and music.
*   **Sustainable Fashion:** Photos of upcycled or eco-friendly clothing designs.

## 3. Submission Flow
**Step 1: The Open Call**
Accessible via the website (`/creative/submit`) and WhatsApp bot.
**Step 2: The Upload**
*   **Creator Details:** Name, Location (City/Village), Social Handles.
*   **The Work:** High-res file upload.
*   **The Story (Crucial):** A 100-word caption explaining the piece. *Why did you create this? What does it mean for Zimbabwe?*
**Step 3: Curation**
Review by the Creative Director/Committee (see Moderation).
**Step 4: Publication**
Live on the Digital Gallery and shared on social media.

## 4. Creator Recognition
We don't just take content; we elevate creators.
*   **"Artist of the Month":** A dedicated feature interview on the homepage and newsletter.
*   **The Climate Gallery:** A permanent digital portfolio page for every accepted artist.
*   **Exhibitions:** Selection for the annual "Art of Adaptation" physical/virtual exhibition.
*   **Micro-Grants (Future Goal):** Small funding for impactful climate art projects.

## 5. Moderation Principles
We are inclusive, but we protect the mission.
*   **Rule #1: Relevance.** Must be connected to the environment, nature, or climate resilience.
*   **Rule #2: No Disinformation.** Artistic license is welcome, but we cannot host content that denies climate change or promotes scientifically harmful practices (e.g., burning plastic).
*   **Rule #3: Respect.** No hate speech, political partisanship, or offensive content.
*   **Rule #4: Originality.** No AI-generated art without disclosure. We prioritize human expression here.

## 6. The "Vibe"
*   **Not:** A sterile museum or a depressing archive of disaster.
*   **Is:** A vibrant, colorful, energetic celebration of resilience. Think "Afrofuturism meets Nature."
