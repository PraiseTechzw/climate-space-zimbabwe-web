# Hurudzai AI | Product Catalog

This document details the suite of intelligent solutions developed under the Hurudzai AI initiative.

## 🌾 Agri-Search Engine (Platform Core)
A localized, AI-powered agricultural advisory engine tuned for Zimbabwe's specific agro-ecological zones.

- **Status**: Beta
- **Key Features**:
  - Localized planting windows.
  - Soil & variety recommendations.
  - Multi-lingual support (English, Shona, Ndebele).
- **Target Audience**: Small-scale farmers, extension workers.

## 💬 Hurudzai WhatsApp Bot
Ensuring climate intelligence is accessible without a high-end smartphone or constant internet access.

- **Status**: Development
- **Capabilities**: 
  - Text-based queries for weather advisories.
  - Pest identification via photo upload.
  - Market price alerts.
- **Integration**: Powered by Twilio/WhatsApp Business API.

## 📊 Farmers AI CRM
A data management platform for organizations working with grassroots farmers.

- **Status**: Concept
- **Purpose**: 
  - Track farmer yields and challenges.
  - Automate SMS notifications for drought alerts.
  - Generate regional impact reports.

## 🛠️ Hurudzai Developer Console
Empowering local developers to build on top of our climate intelligence models.

- **Status**: Planning
- **Features**:
  - API endpoints for weather interpretation.
  - Webhooks for regional alerts.
  - SDK for agricultural app development.

## 🏢 B2B Solutions
Custom intelligence dashboards for NGOs, government agencies, and agribusinesses.

- **Status**: Inquire
- **Offerings**:
  - Longitudinal data analysis.
  - Supply chain risk assessment.
  - Policy impact simulations.
