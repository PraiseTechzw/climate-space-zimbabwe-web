## 2. Page Breakdown

### 1. Home Page (`/`)
*   **Purpose:** The "Hook." Immediately establish the intersection of AI + Creativity + Climate Action.
*   **Key Sections:**
    *   Hero: Impactful visual + "Empowering Resilience through AI & Art".
    *   The Problem vs. Our Solution: Quick snapshot of the mission.
    *   Featured Tools: Direct link to the Agri-Search Engine.
    *   Latest Creative Works: Visual gallery.
    *   Impact Stats: Data-driven proof.
*   **Primary User Action:** Explore Solutions / Join the Movement.

### 2. About Us (`/about`)
*   **Purpose:** Build trust and establish legitimacy.
*   **Sub-pages / Sections:**
    *   **Our Story:** The "Why" (Constitution summary, history).
    *   **Brand DNA:** Mission, Vision, Values.
    *   **Team:** Leadership & structure.
    *   **Partners:** Who supports us (logos).
*   **Primary User Action:** Read our Manifesto / Meet the Team.

### 3. Our Solutions (AI & Tech) (`/solutions`)
*   *Note: Replaces generic "Projects" to focus on the specific AI mandate.*
*   **Purpose:** Provide the actual tools for adaptation.
*   **Sub-pages:**
    *   **Agri-Search Engine:** The core AI tool for farmers (Search insights, weather data).
    *   **Community App:** Info about the knowledge-sharing platform.
    *   **Research Lab:** Ongoing AI research initiatives.
*   **Primary User Action:** Try the Search Engine / Download App.

### 4. Creative Space (`/creative`)
*   *Note: Focuses on the "Creative Engagement" objective.*
*   **Purpose:** Inspire through art and storytelling.
*   **Sections:**
    *   **Digital Gallery:** Climate art, photography, and digital pieces.
    *   **Stories:** Human-interest stories from the frontlines of climate change.
    *   **Events:** Exhibitions and workshops.
*   **Primary User Action:** View Gallery / Submit Art.

### 5. Get Involved (`/get-involved`)
*   **Purpose:** Convert passive visitors into active members.
*   **Sub-pages:**
    *   **For Farmers:** How to access tools.
    *   **For Creatives:** How to contribute art.
    *   **For Partners:** Collaboration opportunities.
    *   **Donate/Support:** Funding info.
*   **Primary User Action:** Sign Up / Contact for Partnership.

### 6. Resources (`/resources`)
*   **Purpose:** Education and data democratization.
*   **Sections:**
    *   **Climate Library:** Simple guides on climate change in Zimbabwe.
    *   **Data Reports:** Downloadable insights.
    *   **News/Blog:** Organization updates.
*   **Primary User Action:** Download a Guide / Read Article.

### 7. Contact (`/contact`)
*   **Purpose:** Direct communication lines.
*   **Sections:**
    *   Contact Form.
    *   Office Location (if physical).
    *   Social Media Links.
*   **Primary User Action:** Send Message.
