# Climate Space Zimbabwe | Voice & Language Guide

## 1. Our Voice
**Human. Intelligent. Optimistic. Action-Oriented.**

We speak to people, not "users" or "beneficiaries." We are the bridge between complex climate science and everyday action. We sound like a knowledgeable partner who is excited about the future, not a distant academic institution.

### The 4 Pillars of Our Voice
1.  **Conversational, not Institutional:** We use contractions ("we're", "let's"). We ask questions. We use active voice.
2.  **Simple, not Simplistic:** We explain complex AI and climate concepts in plain English. We respect the reader's intelligence but don't burden them with jargon.
3.  **Empowering, not Alarming:** We focus on the solution, not just the problem. We acknowledge the crisis but pivot quickly to what can be done.
4.  **Local yet Global:** We use examples relevant to Zimbabwe (maize, drought, local rains) while connecting them to global trends.

## 2. Word Choice

### Words to Use Often (The "Green List")
*   **Action:** Build, Create, Grow, Solve, Adapt, Start.
*   **Connection:** Together, Community, Us, Partners, Share.
*   **Impact:** Resilience, Thriving, Future, Smart, Practical.
*   **Tone:** Simple, Clear, Now, Today.

### Words to Avoid (The "Red List")
*   **Bureaucratic:** Synergize, Leverage (use "use"), Utilise (use "use"), Facilitate (use "help").
*   **Passive/Weak:** "It is believed that...", "Efforts are being made..."
*   **Fear-Mongering:** Catastrophe, Doom, Apocalypse, Irreversible (unless scientifically specific context), Hopeless.
*   **Overly Academic:** Anthropogenic, Mitigation (explain it as "reducing damage"), Adaptation (explain it as "adjusting to change").

## 3. Writing Rules

### Rule #1: Lead with the "Why"
Don't just state a fact. Explain why it matters to the reader.
*   **Bad:** "Climate Space Zimbabwe uses AI to analyze precipitation patterns."
*   **Good:** "We use AI to track rainfall so farmers know exactly when to plant."

### Rule #2: No "Doomscrolling"
Never present a problem without a path to a solution.
*   **Bad:** "Climate change is destroying Zimbabwe's agriculture and causing mass hunger."
*   **Good:** "Changing weather patterns challenge our farmers. That's why we're building tools to predict droughts before they happen."

### Rule #3: Be Specific
Vague promises build no trust. Concrete details do.
*   **Bad:** "We help the environment."
*   **Good:** "We help 500 farmers in Masvingo save water using smart sensors."

## 4. Examples: Good vs. Bad

| :--- | :--- | :--- |
