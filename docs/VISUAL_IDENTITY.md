# Climate Space Zimbabwe | Visual Identity System

## 1. Brand Essence
**Modern. Eco-Tech. African. Impactful.**
The visual identity blends the organic nature of climate action with the precision of Artificial Intelligence. It is rooted in Zimbabwe but built for a global stage.

## 2. Color Palette (Strict Brand Tokens)

### Primary Colors
*   **Official Zimbabwe Green** (`#009E60`): The core brand color. Used for identity, leaves, and primary actions.
*   **Official Zimbabwe Gold** (`#FFD200`): Represents Zimbabwe, the sun, and high-impact elements.
*   **Midnight Charcoal** (`#111827`): The primary dark shade for text, footers, and strong contrast sections.

### Secondary & Accent Colors
*   **Official Cyan** (`#00B4F0`): Represents Artificial Intelligence (AI) and technology.
*   **Official Red** (`#D40000`): Used for alerts, warnings, or emphasis.
*   **Secondary Green** (`#6BCF9B`): A lighter, vibrant green for growth indicators and hover states.
*   **Light Gold** (`#FFE580`): A soft gold for backgrounds and highlights.
*   **Soft Background Green** (`#F0FDF4`): The primary background color for light mode.

### Neutral Colors
*   **Clean White** (`#FFFFFF`): Used for card surfaces and text on dark backgrounds.
*   **Pure Black** (`#000000`): Used for logo backgrounds.

## 3. Typography System

### Typeface 1: Headings
**Font:** `Plus Jakarta Sans`
**Usage:**
*   **H1 (Hero):** Bold / ExtraBold.
*   **H2 (Section):** SemiBold.

### Typeface 2: Body Copy
**Font:** `Inter`
**Usage:**
*   **Body:** Regular (400).
*   **Captions/Labels:** Medium (500).

## 4. Logo Usage Rules
*   **Preferred Backgrounds:** Midnight Charcoal (`#111827`), Pure Black (`#000000`), or White.
*   **Clear Space:** Maintain clear space equal to the height of the 'C'.

## 5. Implementation Notes
*   **Do NOT** use hardcoded hex values.
*   **Do NOT** use default Tailwind colors (e.g., `text-green-600`, `bg-yellow-500`).
*   **ALWAYS** use the `brand-*` tokens:
    *   `bg-brand-green`
    *   `text-brand-gold`
    *   `text-brand-cyan`
    *   `bg-brand-dark`
    *   `bg-brand-light`
