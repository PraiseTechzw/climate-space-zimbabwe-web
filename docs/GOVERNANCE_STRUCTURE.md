# Leadership & Governance Structure

## Philosophy
Transparency, Accountability, and Action. We don't hide behind bureaucracy.

## 1. Organizational Structure (The "Org Tree")

### **The Board (Oversight)**
*   **Role:** Guardians of the Mission.
*   **Responsibilities:**
    *   Approve annual strategy & budget.
    *   Ensure legal & ethical compliance.
    *   Select Executive Director.
*   **Meeting Frequency:** Quarterly.

### **The Executive Committee (Strategy)**
*   **Executive Director:**
    *   Lead the ship.
    *   Public face of the organization.
*   **Director of Operations:**
    *   Make things happen.
    *   Manage finances and logistics.
*   **Director of Technology (AI):**
    *   Oversee technical development.
*   **Director of Creative (Art):**
    *   Manage brand and artistic output.

### **The Departments (Action)**
*   **Research & AI:** Data scientists, agronomists.
*   **Creative Studio:** Designers, writers, filmmakers.
*   **Community Outreach:** Coordinators for clubs and farmers.

## 2. Reporting Structure
*   **Bottom-Up Innovation:** Ideas come from the Community/Departments -> Executive Committee -> Board (for major approvals).
*   **Top-Down Strategy:** Board sets Vision -> Executive Committee creates Plan -> Departments Execute.

## 3. Ethics & Accountability
*   **Financial Transparency:** Annual reports published openly.
*   **Code of Conduct:** Zero tolerance for corruption or harassment.
*   **Conflict of Interest:** All leaders must declare interests annually.
*   **"Open Door" Policy:** Any member can report issues directly to the Board Chair.
